# Tone Exercises use recorded audio, not text-to-speech

Tone Exercises play Tone Recordings of single syllables; text-to-speech is used only for whole Words and sentences. Device speech engines often mispronounce or flatten isolated Mandarin syllables, and many Android devices have no Chinese voice installed, so relying on them would risk teaching beginners the wrong tone in the very part of the course that matters most. Every recording set must have a license compatible with a free app and be listed in the Acknowledgements.
