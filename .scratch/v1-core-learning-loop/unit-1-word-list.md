# Unit 1 "Greetings & introductions": draft Word list

**Status:** approved by the developer as drafted on 2026-11-11 (ticket 10): Words, Lesson titles and 他/她 in one Lesson all kept.

Meanings below are placeholders written for review; when building, each Word's pinyin and meaning come from CC-CEDICT (CC BY-SA 4.0), trimmed to a short beginner gloss. Pinyin uses tone marks; a syllable with no mark is the neutral tone.

## Lesson 1: "The four tones & hello"

Opens with tone Exercises on **ma** (mā má mǎ mà), then the first Words.

| Characters | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 你 | nǐ | you |
| 好 | hǎo | good, well |
| 你好 | nǐ hǎo | hello |
| 我 | wǒ | I, me |

Exercises: tone (ma ×4, both directions), tone (ni, hao), multiple choice ×4, listening (你好).

## Lesson 3: "Who is it?"

| Characters | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 是 | shì | to be (am, is, are) |
| 不 | bù | not, no |
| 他 | tā | he, him |
| 她 | tā | she, her |
| 老师 | lǎoshī | teacher |

Exercises: multiple choice ×5, listening (是, 老师), tone (shi, bu, ta).
Note: 他 and 她 sound the same, so neither gets a listening Exercise.

Tone Exercises use **ji** and **chan** instead of the per-Lesson syllables above (ma, ni, hao, xie, zai, jian, shi, bu, ta, jiao, ming), because those are the only syllables with licensed recordings of all four tones in one voice (ticket 07).
