# 06: Listening Exercises

**What to build:** The Learner gets listening Exercises: they hear a Word spoken and pick its English meaning, and can replay the audio before answering. Audio goes through a small audio player interface; whole Words use the device's speech engine. Tests use a fake player.

**Blocked by:** 01

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Listening Exercise plays the Word and offers English meanings to choose from
- [ ] Replay button plays the Word again
- [ ] Audio player interface with a device speech-engine source for whole Words
- [ ] Fake player in tests records what was asked to play
- [ ] Content builder can define listening Exercises for a Lesson
- [ ] If no Chinese voice is available on the device, the Learner sees a clear message instead of silence
